# Exemples de conversations simulées (V9)

Légende : 🎮 scène pop culture, 😉 clin d’œil pop culture, 🔁 running gag du village, 😄 réaction à une blague du joueur.

## Joueur blagueur

**Jour 1 — Simone Ellis**

- *Simone Ellis* : The barista remembered my order. I'm officially predictable.
- *You* : What's your usual?
- *Simone Ellis* : Black coffee and a cinnamon roll.
- *You* : Thanks for explaining.
- *Simone Ellis* : Happy to help. I’m basically the friendly shopkeeper in the first village. 😉

**Jour 1 — Ben Reed**

- *Ben Reed* : My cat keeps staring at the empty garage.
- *You* : There has to be a story behind that.
- *Ben Reed* : That story has three endings. Depends on what dialogue option you pick. 😉
- *You* : I had to ask.
- *Ben Reed* : I’ll pretend I laughed.

**Jour 1 — Hank Mercer**

- *Hank Mercer* : I heard there may be a reprint, but that’s only a rumor.
- *You* : That sounds like a collector’s nightmare.
- *Hank Mercer* : Oh, that’s a good one. I won’t remember it, but it’s good. 😄
- *Hank Mercer* : Nightmare is a strong word. Accurate, but strong.
- *You* : I’ll stop teasing.
- *Hank Mercer* : Common. Very common.

**Jour 1 — Luis Ortega**

- *Luis Ortega* : I knew I should've stayed in bed.
- *Luis Ortega* : I feel like we just invented a new holiday.
- *You* : It’s a gift.

**Jour 1 — Victor Shaw**
